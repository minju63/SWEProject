What is the Certificate of Alien Registration? 
This certificate is   a civil   service document that allows foreigners to prove their Foreign Resident Registration facts
by applying for the certificate through Immigration Offices, Immigration Service Centers (including branch offices),
or at the offices of townships, towns, or neighborhoods.

When is the Certificate of Alien Registration required? 
In cases where a foreign resident in   South Korea is   required to provide a resident registration card or a certified
copy of resident registration for various legal procedures and transactions under Korean law, they may use the 
“ Foreign   Resident Registration Card ”   or the   “ Certificate   of Alien Registration ”   as a substitute.
